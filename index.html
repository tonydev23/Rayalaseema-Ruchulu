About Us

A Taste of Rayalaseema

Bold spices, homestyle recipes.

Welcome to Rayalaseema Ruchulu, a destination for lovers of authentic Andhra and Rayalaseema flavours, hearty portions and comforting Indian dining.

Located in Ameerpet, Hyderabad, Rayalaseema Ruchulu brings regional specialities together with South Indian favourites, biryani, desserts and beverages.

From Gongura Mamsam and Gongura Mutton to Natu Kodi Pulusu, Ragi Sangiti and Seafood Thali, the menu celebrates the bold flavours of Andhra and Rayalaseema.
